The woman you’ve become

From the little girl I met to this still small, to be fair, woman.
But you’ve grown in the most important of ways. I’m very proud of the woman you’ve become.

Happy birthday to the pocket-sized disaster I’m lucky to call my person 😂

You’re short, yes. But you’re also the biggest proof that the best things come in 5-foot-nothing packages...

You’re clumsy, also true. Gravity just really likes you, huh?...

And bad luck? Nah. The only bad luck in your life is everyone else...

From 2018 till now you’ve been the one I can always count on...

May your year be soft, loud, and exactly how you want it...

Love you lots, you lilac-loving, short-stacked, clumsy queen.
– Migcobo

P.S. 7 days late, 0 regrets. You’re worth the wait. Also duck when you walk through doorframes.
